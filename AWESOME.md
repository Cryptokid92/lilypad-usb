## How this repo fits

`lilypad-usb` is a **catalog + generator** for SparkFun LilyPad USB (`1b4f:9208`).
Payloads are fun / convenience sketches for **your own** Omarchy/Hyprland lab.
Not a Duckyscript runtime. Convert ideas from the lists above into `catalog.json` ops, then regenerate.
